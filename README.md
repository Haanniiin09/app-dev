# Falling Into Your Smile

![picture](https://images.app.goo.gl/ZL2GxW4DZzckaPBUA)

## Overview
> Falling Into Your Smile is a Chinese drama that follows Tong Yao, a female e-sports player who joins the all-male ZGDX team. She becomes the first female player in the Chinese Pro League, facing challenges and growing both personally and professionally. The series explores her journey to prove herself, her blossoming romance with the team's captain Lu Sicheng, and the team's pursuit of the world championship.

## Character Description
|Character|Description|
|---------|-----------|

## Plot
"Falling Into Your Smile" follows the journey of Tong Yao, a talented amateur gamer who breaks into the male-dominated world of professional League of Legends by joining the ZGDX team. As the league's first female pro player, she faces skepticism while striving to prove her skills and earn the respect of her teammates, particularly the aloof and highly skilled captain, Lu Si Cheng. Despite her vow against dating within the industry, a compelling romance blossoms between Tong Yao and Lu Si Cheng amidst the pressures of competition, teamwork, and personal growth, ultimately showcasing her triumph in the e-sports arena and the unexpected development of their relationship
